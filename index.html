Just ask us
How do I submit my music to EeZee Conceptz?

Send us a message below and choose “Demo submission”, or book a meeting with our team. Tell us who you are, share links to your music and what you are looking for.

Can I book an artiste for my church or event?

Yes. Our artistes minister at worship nights and conferences, concerts and tours, and community outreach programmes. Share your date and details through the booking form.

Can I use your studio if I’m not signed to the label?

Studio sessions, video production, promotion and consultation can be booked directly with our in-house team. Tell us about your project and we’ll recommend the right service.

Where can I listen to your releases?

Everywhere you stream: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Amazon Music, Audiomack, Deezer, Boomplay, Tidal and SoundCloud.

Where are you based?

EeZee Conceptz Limited is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission in Nigeria, with an office at 110 W Randol Mill Rd, Ste 230, Arlington, TX 76011. We work with artistes and partners worldwide.
